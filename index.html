About

Before joining UC Merced, I completed my M.S. in Computer Science at California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo, where I worked with Prof. Franz Kurfess on multi-agent AI systems for search and rescue, human-AI peer review, and AI for engineering education.

I received my B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering from Northeastern University, where I worked with Prof. Yanfeng Zhang on distributed graph neural network training systems.

I also served as a teaching assistant for CSC 580: Intelligent Agents / Artificial Intelligence at Cal Poly.
